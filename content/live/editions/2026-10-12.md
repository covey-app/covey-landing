Halloween has taken over the calendar: vampires at the Balboa, a haunted house inside the Old Mint, and a costume challenge at the Sunset Mercantile.

It's also a big weekend to be outside. Potrero Hill throws its 51st R&B festival, Sunday Streets closes Mission Street in the Excelsior, and Market Street turns into one long arts festival for a night. Check the weather plan before you pick.
