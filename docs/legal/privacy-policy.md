## 4. CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL INFORMATION COLLECTED

**4.1 Account information.** Your email address and the account identifier issued
by your authentication method, being an email-and-password credential, Sign in
with Apple, or Sign in with Google. Where you elect to use Apple's Private Email
Relay, Covey receives the relay address in place of your email address.

**4.2 Profile information.** Display name, username, profile photograph,
biography, home city, occupation, personality-type indicator, and social media
links you elect to publish.

**4.3 Preference information.** Activities, cuisines, dietary restrictions, food
preferences, media genres, fashion styles, sports and self-declared skill levels,
nightlife preferences, and outdoor preferences, each selected by you during
onboarding or subsequently.

**4.4 Date of birth and gender.** Collected for the purposes of enforcing the
minimum age requirement and of applying the age and gender criteria that Plan
hosts and Users of Dating Features specify. Stored separately from your published
profile and not disclosed to other Users.

**4.5 Identity verification information — Sensitive Personal Information.** A
photographic image of a government-issued identity document and a facial
photograph, together with the status and timestamps of the verification. Covey's
personnel compare the two images visually. **Covey does not perform automated
facial recognition and does not generate, derive, or store any biometric template,
faceprint, or other mathematical representation of facial geometry.** These images
are stored in a private, access-controlled repository accessible only to their
subject and to Covey's authorised reviewers, are never displayed on any profile,
and are never disclosed to other Users.

**4.6 Dating information — Sensitive Personal Information.** Where, and only
where, you enable Dating Features, Covey collects your gender identity, the
descriptive label you apply to your sexual orientation, the genders to which you
are attracted, the age range you specify, your height, and your self-reported
exercise frequency. Dating Features are disabled by default.

**4.7 User Content.** Plans and their particulars, including the name, address,
and coordinates of each location added to a Plan; invitations, attendance, and
join requests; posts and recaps; photographs and other media uploaded to profiles,
Plans, posts, and conversations; messages in Plan conversations and direct
messages; groups created or joined; bookmarks and saved places; and comments.

**4.8 Trust and safety information.** Reports you submit, the stated reason and
any evidence attached; blocks you apply; and moderation determinations and their
records.

**4.9 Support and feedback information.** The contents of your correspondence with
Covey.

**4.10 Usage and diagnostic information.** Named application events with an
associated session identifier, application version, and device type; screens
viewed and features used; and crash reports, error traces, and performance
measurements.

**4.11 Connection information.** Internet Protocol address and request timestamps,
recorded by Covey's infrastructure in order to serve requests, apply rate limits,
and detect abuse. An Internet Protocol address yields an approximate geographic
region of city-level granularity or coarser. It does not yield device geolocation.

**4.12 Notification information.** Where you enable notifications, the Apple Push
Notification service device token, together with a device identifier, application
version, and locale.

**4.13 Consent records.** The version of each policy document you accepted, the
date and time of acceptance, and the registration method used.

**4.14 Website information.** The waitlist form at coveyapp.co collects an email
address, first name, city, and your confirmation that you are eighteen (18) years
of age or older. The contact form collects a name, email address, subject, and
message.

## 8. AUTOMATED GENERATION OF TEXT

8.1 Certain optional features generate suggested text: a profile biography, a
draft Plan, a group description, conversation prompts, and the interpretation of
free-text search queries. These features transmit data to the Gemini application
programming interface operated by Google LLC.

8.2 **Data transmitted.** Only the data necessary for the requested feature is
transmitted, being typically the preference selections you made during onboarding,
the text you have entered, or the Plan you are drafting.

8.3 **Data not transmitted.** Covey does not transmit to the model provider your
identity verification information, your date of birth, your dating information,
your direct messages, or your email address.

8.4 **Server-side execution.** Requests are issued by Covey's servers and not by
your device. The model provider does not receive your Covey account identity and
Covey's credentials are not exposed to the application.

8.5 Model Output is a suggestion, is not reviewed by any person before
presentation, and may be inaccurate. Section 8 of the Terms of Service governs
your use of it.
