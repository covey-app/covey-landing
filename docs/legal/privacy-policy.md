## 8. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

8.1 **Provider.** Covey's AI features and its automated safety checks use the
Gemini application programming interface operated by Google LLC ("**Google**").
Requests are sent by Covey's servers, not by your device.

8.2 **Consent before any AI feature.** Before any of the features in Section 8.3
sends information about you to Google, the application shows you a screen naming
Google Gemini and describing what is sent, and asks for your agreement. Nothing
is sent until you agree. If you decline, the features remain unavailable and the
rest of the Service works normally. You may withdraw your agreement at any time
in the application's settings, after which no further requests are sent. Covey
records each agreement and withdrawal (Section 4.13). Where Covey materially
changes what is sent, it will ask again.

8.3 **Features and the data each sends.** Each feature sends only the following:

| Feature | Data sent to Google |
|---|---|
| Profile biography suggestion | The activities, activity categories, cuisines, media genres, and fashion styles you have selected |
| Plan co-pilot | The request you type; the Plan date; an Approximate Area and town or neighbourhood name (Section 7.1.2); your selected preferences; and, for up to five Users you mention who have themselves agreed to Covey's AI features, their selected preferences, with their names replaced by placeholders such as "Friend 1" |
| Explore search | The search text you type |
| Covey Group description | The group's name and privacy setting, and up to twelve activity labels drawn from its members' preferences, without their names |
| Conversation ice-breakers | The activity interests the participants have in common and the number of participants, without their names and without any message |
| Onboarding suggestions | The description of your interests you type during onboarding |

8.4 **Data not sent by these features.** Where a feature involves other Users,
their names are replaced with placeholders. The features in Section 8.3 do not
send your name, email address, telephone number, date of birth, dating
information, optional profile details under Section 4.16, identity verification
information, or the contents of your messages. Google receives the request from
Covey and not from your Account, and Covey does not send Google your account
identifier.

8.5 **Google's use of the data.** Covey uses Gemini on Google's paid service
terms, under which Google does not use prompts or responses to train or improve
its models. Google may retain them for a limited period to detect abuse and to
meet legal obligations, under those terms. Covey does not store the text of AI
requests, other than briefly in memory to answer repeated identical requests.

> **8.6 MODEL OUTPUT.** Model Output is a suggestion, is not reviewed by any
> person before it is presented to you, and may be inaccurate. You decide
> whether to use it. Section 8 of the Terms of Service governs your use of it.

8.7 **Automated safety checks.** To enforce the Terms of Service and keep Users
safe, Covey sends the following User Content to Google's Gemini service for
automated classification against Covey's content rules: profile biographies;
Plan titles and descriptions and the text of Plan stops; Covey Group names and
descriptions; recap captions; messages in direct, Plan, and group conversations;
and images, including profile photographs, other profile photographs, Plan and
Covey Group cover photographs, photographs sent in conversations, and recap
photographs. Covey sends the content to be checked, not your name, email address,
or profile. These checks are part of operating the Service safely and are not
subject to the consent in Section 8.2; they are described in the Terms of
Service. Section 8.5 applies to them.

8.8 **Effect of a safety check.** Content that a check classifies as high risk
is hidden automatically and placed in a queue for review by a person, who decides
whether it breaches the Terms of Service. No Account is suspended or banned by an
automated check alone. If you believe content of yours was hidden in error, you
may ask for it to be reviewed under Section 12.5 of the Terms of Service.
